- **A loading screen while a page loads.** Opening an issue, a link or an address now covers the whole browser with Kehikot's own loading screen, saying "Loading content…" and what is being opened, until the new page is there. A quick hop inside a site is not covered.
- **A page that will not load says so.** If a page fails, the screen says it could not be loaded and why, with Try again. One still loading after 30 seconds says it is taking a long time. Either way you can choose to see the page as it is.
- **Back, Forward and Reload are in the container's footer.** In a Kehikot that draws module footers, the browser's buttons, the page's title and Save address sit along the bottom edge, and only the address field stays at the top. Reload turns into Stop while a page loads. Older Kehikot versions keep the bar at the top as before.
- **Smoother to watch and lighter on the computer.** The page's picture reaches the screen as smaller messages and is decoded in the background, so scrolling and typing respond sooner. A browser on a kehikko you have left stops sending pictures until you come back.
