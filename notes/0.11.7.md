Kind: feature

- **Going back is instant.** The page of each space you visit stays ready in the background, so switching back shows it at once, scrolled and typed into as you left it.
- **Light on your computer.** Pages kept ready are paused while out of sight, so they use no processor. Browser keeps fewer of them when your computer is short of memory.
- **Fresh without flashing.** A page you come back to is checked with the site. If it changed, it is loaded again out of sight and swapped in when ready, so you never see a blank page.
- **Never loses what you typed.** A page with unsaved input is never reloaded by itself. The footer says when a newer version is available, and you choose when to reload it.
- **Settings if you want them.** Choose how many pages to keep ready, their memory, when and how they refresh, and whether an empty space suggests pages. Every setting is Automatic by default.
