- **Opens pages other modules hand it.** When another module on the kehikko, or an agent, has a link that is not an issue or a pull request, such as a design document, a preview, a dashboard or a pipeline log, it now opens here instead of in a new browser tab.
- **Says who asked, and lets you keep it.** The line under the address bar names the page and who handed it over, and "Save address" adds it to your saved addresses.
- **Opens each one once.** Reloading Browser does not open the same page again, and a page you were sent is not replaced by the address remembered for the place.
